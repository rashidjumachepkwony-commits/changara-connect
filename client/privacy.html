Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you register an account, list a business, post a product, or contact us. This includes your name, phone number, email address, location, and any content you choose to publish.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate and improve the platform, process your listings, facilitate communication between users, send important notifications, and ensure the security of the platform.

3. Public Content

Listings, reviews, and other content you submit become publicly visible. Please do not share personal information of others without their consent.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

You can update or delete your profile information at any time from your dashboard. You can also contact us to request data deletion in accordance with applicable law.

6. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at Contact Us or email info@changaraconnect.co.ke.
