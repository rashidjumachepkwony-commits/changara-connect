About Changara Connect

Find It. Connect Locally. Grow Together.

Changara Connect is a local marketplace and services platform dedicated to connecting people, businesses, and opportunities across Changara and the wider Teso North area in Busia County, Kenya.

Our mission is simple: help locals find what they need, promote their businesses, and discover opportunities right in their own community. Whether you are looking for a phone repair shop, a house to rent, a job, or a place to sell your goods, Changara Connect brings you closer.

Every listing on our platform goes through a review process to help keep the community safe and trustworthy. We believe in the power of local connections and the potential of our community.
